About Kutan Nala Waterfall

Kutan Nala Waterfall is a breathtaking natural wonder located in the scenic Balod district. Nestled amidst dense forests and hilly terrains, this waterfall offers a serene escape from the hustle and bustle of city life. With its crystal-clear waters cascading down from a great height, Kutan Nala is a perfect destination for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts alike.

Activities

  • Picnicking: Enjoy a peaceful day out with family and friends.
  • Trekking: Explore the surrounding forests and hills.
  • Photography: Capture the stunning beauty of the waterfall and its surroundings.
  • Bird Watching: Observe the diverse avian species in the area.

Visitor Guidelines

Do's

  • Carry sufficient drinking water and snacks.
  • Follow the designated trekking paths for safety.
  • Keep the environment clean by disposing of waste responsibly.
  • Wear comfortable and sturdy shoes.

Don'ts

  • Avoid littering and polluting the waterfall area.
  • Do not engage in dangerous activities near the waterfall edge.
  • Avoid swimming in deep waters without safety gear.
  • Do not disturb the local wildlife or plants.
