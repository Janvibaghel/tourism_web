Taj Mahal

A UNESCO World Heritage Site

About the Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, India, is an ivory-white marble mausoleum built in memory of Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan's wife, Mumtaz Mahal. It is widely regarded as one of the finest examples of Mughal architecture.

Distance from Major Cities

  • Delhi: 210 km
  • Jaipur: 240 km
  • Lucknow: 320 km

What to Do There

  • Explore the stunning architecture and intricate carvings.
  • Take a guided tour to learn about the history.
  • Enjoy a peaceful walk in the surrounding gardens.
  • Visit at sunrise or sunset for breathtaking views.

Nearby Attractions

  • Agra Fort - 2.5 km away
  • Fatehpur Sikri - 40 km away
  • Itimad-ud-Daulah's Tomb - 5 km away
