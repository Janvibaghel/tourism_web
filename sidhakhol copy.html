About Siddhakhol Waterfall

Siddhakhol Waterfall is a hidden gem nestled in the heart of nature, offering a serene escape for those who seek tranquility. The waterfall cascades down from a significant height, surrounded by lush greenery and stunning landscapes. It is the perfect destination for nature lovers and adventurers.

Do's

  • Carry your own water and food supplies.
  • Respect the environment by cleaning up after yourself.
  • Wear comfortable footwear for trekking.
  • Follow the marked trails to ensure safety.

Don'ts

  • Do not litter or damage the natural surroundings.
  • Avoid swimming in areas with strong currents.
  • Do not disturb the wildlife or plants.
  • Avoid visiting during heavy rains or storms.
